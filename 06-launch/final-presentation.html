Slide 5 · Strategy

BU Collection – A Fair Way Forward

Problem hook

Today's journey loses money and agent capacity at two points: before an agreement is reached, and after it. Left unchanged, costs rise with volume, portfolio value erodes, and the headline metric (SSR) can mask the problem.

Value proposition

Create a transparent, guided repayment journey that helps consumers choose sustainable instalment plans the first time, reducing unnecessary agent interactions while improving long-term recovery performance beyond SSR.

Data-backed hypothesis

Based on the fact that 46% of started journeys end in an agreement, 31% of starters contact an agent within 7 days, 28% of uploaded income documents need clarification, 19% of new agreements miss a payment within 60 days, and early qualitative themes, I believe that replacing the single monthly-amount commitment with a guided journey for time-poor consumers with variable income and one or more open claims in the German pilot segment, will result in more instalment plans that people can actually keep, set up without an agent, as measured by a 20% relative reduction (19% to about 15%) in the 60-day missed-payment rate of new agreements.

Slide 6 · Research

Future-state journey map: a repayment plan Mara can keep

Three competitive advantages over the manual workaround
  • Answers consequence questions in-journey instead of Google -> Mara commits without leaving the portal.
  • Accepts variable income as a range -> plans match reality, so fewer missed payments.
  • Hands agents full context on escalation -> no repeated checks, faster resolution.
Persona · Mara

A consumer with limited time and uncertain income, dealing with a debt online.

Workaround outside the product

Google "what happens if I miss a payment", a spreadsheet or notes app for her own budget, family and free debt-advice services, hunting for payslips, or an agent call where she repeats everything.

Friction

She is asked to commit to a monthly amount she cannot predict, and fears one wrong answer or one missed payment will make everything due at once. So she under-reports, abandons, or calls for reassurance.

PRD highlights · "What if I miss an instalment."
  • Direct answer to the core fear, in plain language. First line states what happens if they miss one instalment, including whether the full balance becomes due. Short, non-legal wording, ideally under about 50 words.
  • Verified copy. Policy, Legal and Compliance confirm every claim for each creditor and market in scope.
  • Placement at the commitment point. Beside the amount choice, visible by default, no click-through, no modal. Also on the summary step.
  • One honest next step if they can't pay. A single line points to the route that exists today, such as contacting support.
  • Readable on a small screen. Legible and accessible on mobile.
Guardrail

Plans that miss a payment within 60 days must not increase above the 19% baseline.
