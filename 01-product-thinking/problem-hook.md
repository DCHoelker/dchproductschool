# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** BU Collection - A Fair Way Forward
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Over a year, that is roughly 54,000 abandoned journeys, 31,000 avoidable agent contacts and 8,800 failed agreements. With over half of agent workload already tied to payment, legal and enforcement processes, the cost to serve stays high and does not scale.

The likely effect on market position:

Cost and margin. Competitors and clients' other collection partners roll out consistent digital self-service, and Riverty stays dependent on agents.
Client trust. Clients increasingly expect compliant, transparent, digital servicing. Without it, Riverty's collection mandates become harder to win and keep.
Purchased portfolios. Riverty carries the risk itself, so plans that fail at 19% steadily erode portfolio value, and data quality problems go unaddressed.
Regulatory and reputational exposure. A fragmented journey that leaves consumers confused about consequences is a fairness and compliance liability. The expansion across European markets would also stall without a reusable core.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Mara is on her phone between shifts, with variable income, two claims and no sense of what is affordable. She is likely to:

Google "what happens if I miss a payment" or "can I negotiate a debt".
Work out her own budget in a spreadsheet or notes app, because the portal doesn't show what a sustainable amount looks like.
Ask family or friends, or consult free debt-advice services, about whether the letter is legitimate and what the risks are.
Hunt for payslips and bank statements she can't easily find, then give up on the upload.
Call an agent, wait on hold, and repeat everything she already entered.
Or do nothing, which leaves two cases drifting towards escalation.

Daniel works around the journey too. He re-asks identity, affordability and case questions, hunts through several systems and past letters for context, and manually reviews the 28% of income documents that need clarification. He has no explanation of why the system recommended or escalated something, and no easy way to correct it.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Bottom line: Today's journey loses money and agent capacity at two points: before an agreement is reached, and after it. Left unchanged, costs rise with volume, portfolio value erodes, and the headline metric (SSR) can mask the problem.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Create a transparent, guided repayment journey that helps consumers choose sustainable instalment plans the first time, reducing unnecessary agent interactions while improving long-term recovery performance beyond SSR.
